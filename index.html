FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the product and billing.

What is EqualDocs?

EqualDocs is a new model for AI-native legal services: the legal front door for business. AI handles routine legal work such as questions, drafting and contract review at software economics; licensed counsel steps in when professional judgment is required, whether that is your own lawyer, your legal team or counsel through EqualDocs where available. EqualDocs is also building continuous monitoring and company legal context. The software does not practise law: legal services come from the firm you engage.

Is EqualDocs a law firm?

No. EqualDocs is a technology platform. Legal services are provided by counsel you engage: in Québec, that can be EqualDocs Avocats inc., a law firm authorized to practise in Québec; elsewhere, independent licensed partner firms or your own lawyers. Using the software does not by itself create a lawyer-client relationship. Representation begins only after conflict clearance and an engagement. The AI does not practise law.

Can AI draft an NDA for me?

Yes. EqualDocs can draft NDAs, employment contracts, service agreements, and other common business contracts using AI. Describe what you need in plain language, and the AI generates a complete, professionally formatted document with appropriate legal clauses.

How does AI contract review work?

Upload your contract (PDF or DOCX) and EqualDocs AI analyzes every clause for risks, missing provisions, and compliance issues. You receive a detailed risk report with severity ratings, legal citations, and suggested improvements, typically in under 30 seconds.

Is EqualDocs free?

Yes, you can start for free. The Starter plan includes a monthly allowance of Equal Credits (EC), no credit card required, enough to ask questions and try AI features like drafting and review. Earn more EC (e.g. by completing your profile) or upgrade for higher usage. Legal services delivered by a lawyer are quoted separately and upfront.

Will my data be used to train models?

Not unless you choose to. By default your content is not used to train our AI. Matter content shared with a lawyer, identity documents and your Playbook are never used for training, and no setting allows it. You can opt in to share self-serve chats and documents to improve our AI under Settings → AI & your data, and you keep full ownership of your documents and their IP.

Which file types are supported?

EqualDocs supports DOCX and PDF files. Images embedded in documents are OCR-processed automatically. You can also create documents directly in the platform using AI.

Why choose EqualDocs?

Because EqualDocs starts where your business does: with the business event, not a legal matter. AI prepares documents, checks them and flags risk at software economics; professionals, whether your own or counsel through EqualDocs where available, step in where judgment is required, and legal work is quoted before it begins. Company legal context and the Legal Firewall are being built so that each matter does not start from zero.
